Our Best Services

Probate & Estate Administration

We guide executors and administrators through the New Jersey probate process, including preparation of Form A-1/A-2 petitions, representation in surrogate's court, and resolution of creditor claims against the estate.

International Estate Planning

Our multilingual team specializes in German inheritance certificates (Erbschein), cross-border tax compliance for multinational estates, and facilitating asset transfers between EU countries and the United States.

Estate Litigation

We provide experienced representation in contested estate matters, including will contests, fiduciary duty disputes between executors and beneficiaries, and cases involving claims of undue influence or lack of testamentary capacity.

Asset Protection

Our comprehensive asset protection services include trust formation for wealth preservation, strategic real property title transfers to minimize tax exposure, and business succession planning for family-owned enterprises.
