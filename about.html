About Us

Our Mission

At Wilson & Associates, our mission is to provide exceptional legal guidance through complex estate transitions with integrity, personalized attention, and technical excellence. We are committed to protecting our clients' legacies by navigating the intricate legal frameworks of probate administration and international inheritance law with precision and care.

We strive to make the estate administration process transparent and manageable for our clients during challenging times, while ensuring their assets are protected and distributed according to their wishes across jurisdictions.

Our Vision

We envision Wilson & Associates as the premier resource for individuals and families facing complex estate matters, particularly those with multinational components. Our goal is to bridge legal systems across borders, creating seamless solutions for clients with assets and interests in both the United States and Europe.
